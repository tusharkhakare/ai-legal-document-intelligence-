# WARRANTIES AND REPRESENTATIONS KNOWLEDGE BASE
# TOTAL ENTRIES - 04

TITLE: Express Service and Performance Warranties

CATEGORY: WARRANTIES

REFERENCE_ID: POL-WAR-001

JURISDICTION: Global including India, UK, USA

GUIDANCE:
Vendors and service providers must warrant that all services, deliverables, and software will be performed in a professional, workmanlike manner adhering to highest industry standards, and will conform materially to agreed specifications and documentation for at least ninety (90) days following acceptance.

---

TITLE: Anti-Bribery, Anti-Corruption, and Legal Compliance Warranty

CATEGORY: WARRANTIES

REFERENCE_ID: POL-WAR-002

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Each party must expressly represent and warrant compliance with all applicable anti-bribery, anti-money laundering, anti-corruption regulations (e.g., UK Bribery Act, US FCPA, Indian Prevention of Corruption Act), and the Tata Code of Conduct.

---

TITLE: Disclaimers of Implied Warranties Standards

CATEGORY: WARRANTIES

REFERENCE_ID: POL-WAR-003

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Broad, unilateral warranty disclaimers (e.g., "AS IS, WHERE IS" with total disclaimer of fitness for particular purpose or merchantability) presented by commercial suppliers must be resisted for mission-critical procurement contracts.

---

TITLE: Remedy for Breach of Warranty

CATEGORY: WARRANTIES

REFERENCE_ID: POL-WAR-004

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Upon timely notice of a warranty breach or defect, the vendor must promptly repair, replace, or re-perform the defective deliverable at no additional cost. Failure to cure within thirty (30) days must entitle Tata to terminate and claim a full refund.

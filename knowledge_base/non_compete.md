# NON-COMPETE AND RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS KNOWLEDGE BASE
# TOTAL ENTRIES - 04

TITLE: Reasonable Scope and Geographic Limitations

CATEGORY: NON_COMPETE

REFERENCE_ID: POL-NC-001

JURISDICTION: Global including India, UK, USA

GUIDANCE:
Non-compete clauses must be narrowly tailored in geographic reach, duration (ordinarily 6 to 12 months post-termination), and scope of prohibited activities. Overly broad covenants may be rendered void and unenforceable under applicable jurisdictional laws (e.g., Section 27 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872).

---

TITLE: Non-Solicitation of Employees and Key Personnel

CATEGORY: NON_COMPETE

REFERENCE_ID: POL-NC-002

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Agreements should include mutual non-solicitation covenants restraining either party from actively recruiting, soliciting, or hiring the other party's key employees or engineering staff directly involved in project performance during the term and for 12 months thereafter, excluding general public advertisements.

---

TITLE: Protection of Customer and Client Relationships

CATEGORY: NON_COMPETE

REFERENCE_ID: POL-NC-003

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Vendors and consultants must not divert, solicit, or entice away existing clients or prospective enterprise customers whose identities or trade connections were disclosed or acquired through the contractual engagement.

---

TITLE: Exclusivity and Non-Poaching Standards

CATEGORY: NON_COMPETE

REFERENCE_ID: POL-NC-004

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Exclusivity commitments must define clear boundaries to ensure fair market competition while safeguarding legitimate business secrets, customer lists, and proprietary vendor networks.

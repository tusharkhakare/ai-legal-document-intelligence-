TITLE: Standard Force Majeure Definition and Exclusions

CATEGORY: FORCE_MAJEURE

REFERENCE_ID: POL-FM-001

JURISDICTION: Global including India, UK, USA

GUIDANCE:
Force majeure must cover genuinely unforeseeable, unavoidable events beyond reasonable control (natural disasters, declared wars, government embargoes). Routine market disruptions, commercial impracticability, supplier insolvency, currency fluctuations, or labor shortages must be explicitly excluded from force majeure relief.

---

TITLE: Prompt Written Notice and Mitigation Duty

CATEGORY: FORCE_MAJEURE

REFERENCE_ID: POL-FM-002

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
The affected party must provide prompt written notification within a strict window (typically 3 to 5 business days) specifying the cause, expected duration, and impact. The affected party remains obligated to exercise all commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate delay and resume performance as rapidly as feasible.

---

TITLE: Extended Force Majeure Termination Right

CATEGORY: FORCE_MAJEURE

REFERENCE_ID: POL-FM-003

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
If a force majeure event continues unabated for more than thirty (30) to sixty (60) consecutive days, the unaffected party must have the unilateral right to terminate the contract without penalty or early termination fees, receiving immediate pro-rata refund for prepaid unperformed services.

---

TITLE: Exclusion of Payment Obligations from Force Majeure

CATEGORY: FORCE_MAJEURE

REFERENCE_ID: POL-FM-004

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Force majeure clauses must never excuse accrued monetary debts or ongoing payment obligations for goods or services successfully received prior to the force majeure occurrence.

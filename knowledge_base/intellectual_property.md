# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY KNOWLEDGE BASE
# TOTAL ENTRIES - 04

TITLE: Pre-Existing Background Intellectual Property Protection

CATEGORY: INTELLECTUAL_PROPERTY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-IP-001

JURISDICTION: Global including India, UK, USA, Australia

GUIDANCE:
Each party retains sole ownership of its background intellectual property, proprietary tools, pre-existing software, methodologies, and know-how. No license to background IP is granted unless explicitly stated in writing. Contracts must prevent inadvertent assignment or loss of pre-existing core assets.

---

TITLE: Work Product and Foreground Intellectual Property Ownership

CATEGORY: INTELLECTUAL_PROPERTY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-IP-002

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Custom deliverables, bespoke developments, and work product created specifically for Tata and paid for under the agreement must vest exclusively with Tata upon creation. Vendors may be granted a limited, non-exclusive license only to the extent strictly required to perform services.

---

TITLE: Third-Party and Open Source Software Compliance

CATEGORY: INTELLECTUAL_PROPERTY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-IP-003

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Suppliers must not embed, link, or incorporate copyleft or restrictive open-source software (such as GPL, AGPL) into proprietary deliverables without prior written consent. Suppliers warrant that all deliverables are free from unauthorized third-party code or license encumbrances.

---

TITLE: Intellectual Property Infringement Indemnification

CATEGORY: INTELLECTUAL_PROPERTY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-IP-004

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Vendors and service providers must provide full indemnification against any third-party claims, lawsuits, or demands asserting patent, copyright, trademark, or trade secret infringement arising from deliverables or software provided. IP infringement indemnity must remain uncapped.
